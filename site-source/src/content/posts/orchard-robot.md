## Overview
May 2024 - May 2025 · **Team Lead**

Coordinated project development and team collaboration, contributed to mechanical design, and integrated hardware and software for orchard inspection and targeted spraying.

## Control & communication
Developed an STM32-based vehicle control system for basic motion and precision spraying. Independently integrated and debugged LoRa communication, addressed signal interference, and enabled stable data exchange with the host application.

## Computer vision
Contributed to YOLOv8 pest and disease model training and lightweight deployment for monitoring young pomelo trees, and participated in designing the interactive web interface.

## Outcome
Selected for a university student innovation training project and completed ahead of schedule with an excellent rating.
