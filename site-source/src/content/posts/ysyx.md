## Software: NEMU
Implemented an RV32I instruction-set simulator in C, including clock, keyboard, and VGA device emulation. Added expression evaluation, watchpoints, and support for running CPU-test programs.

## Hardware: A single-cycle processor
Implemented a single-cycle minirv processor in Verilog, covering instruction fetch, decode, execution, memory access, and write-back. Verified functionality with CPU-test and Spike differential testing, and used Logisim to check the datapath and control logic.
